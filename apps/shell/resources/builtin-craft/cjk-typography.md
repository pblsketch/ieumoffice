# CJK Typography Craft

- Never apply synthetic italic (`italic: true` / `font-style: italic`) to Korean, Japanese, or Chinese body runs; use weight, spacing, or subtle accent color for emphasis instead.
- Prefer clean CJK-capable system fonts (`Malgun Gothic`, `Apple SD Gothic Neo`, `Noto Sans KR`) with balanced line-height (`1.5`-`1.7`) and `word-break: keep-all` where supported.
- Avoid cramped letter-spacing or oversized tracking on Hangul syllables.
- Keep mixed Latin/number and CJK text visually aligned with consistent baseline and clear spacing around units.
