# Typography Craft

- Use a clear, disciplined scale: one primary display heading, clear section headers, readable body copy, and muted metadata captions.
- Keep line lengths comfortable (55-75 characters for Latin prose, 32-44 characters per line for Korean/CJK prose) with generous line-height (1.45-1.65 for body).
- Limit font families to at most two complementary local/system faces; never load external font CDNs.
- Keep emphasis purposeful: reserve bold for structural anchors and key figures, not entire paragraphs.
